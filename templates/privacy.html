Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expense data you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what is needed to provide the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Spendly. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and allowing you to filter and manage your transactions.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core functionality of the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit the app. No personal or financial data is shared with Google or any other third party.

We do not integrate with analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external tracking services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to addressing any concerns promptly and transparently.
